About the arcade

Bright, detailed, and built to feel like a tiny browser arcade.

The games are all plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, which keeps them fast to load and easy to extend later.

A survival arena with mouse aim, quick movement, health drops, and a timer that counts down to dawn.
